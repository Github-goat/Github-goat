## 📚 Resources I Recommend
Here are some tools, books, and communities that have helped me grow in cybersecurity—and I hope they help you too!  
- **Books**:  
  - *The Art of Intrusion* by Kevin Mitnick – A must-read for understanding real-world attacks.  
  - *Practical Cloud Security* by Chris Dotson – Great for cloud security beginners.  
- **Tools**:  
  - [TryHackMe](https://tryhackme.com) – Hands-on labs for learning cybersecurity skills.  
  - [MITRE ATT&CK Navigator](https://mitre-attack.github.io/attack-navigator/) – Map threats and build detection strategies.  
- **Communities**:  
  - [Simply Cyber](https://simplycyber.io) – A fantastic resource for free cybersecurity content, daily threat briefings, and career advice. I’m an active member of the Simply Cyber community, where I engage with their livestreams and connect with peers to stay updated on the latest cyber trends.  
  - [Women in Cybersecurity (WiCyS)](https://www.wicys.org) – Amazing for networking and mentorship.  
  - [Black Cybersecurity Association](https://blackcybersecurityassociation.org) – A supportive space for underrepresented professionals.
